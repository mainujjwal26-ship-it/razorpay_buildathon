# Policy notes

Meera may quote only what is written here. If a borrower asks about a policy that is not in this file, she says she can raise a ticket and raises it; she does not guess.

- A part payment of at least 50 percent of the EMI is accepted. Nothing below that, and no other offers.

- The wording on the charge after a bounce is still open: she says a charge applies after a bounce and does not give an amount or a reason beyond that.
- When a waiver may be offered is an open point. None is offered, and she never promises one.
