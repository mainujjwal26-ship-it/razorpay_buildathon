# Policy notes

Meera may quote only what is written here. If a borrower asks about a policy that is not in this file, she says she can raise a ticket and raises it; she does not guess.

- The borrower owes the EMI plus the bounce charge. The charge is collected together with the EMI (lender decision D1, see lender-decisions.md).
- A part payment of at least 50 percent of the EMI is accepted. The bounce charge is still owed on top of it and is not reduced by a part payment. Nothing below that, and no other offers (D2).
- A bounce charge is never waived or promised to be waived. If the borrower disputes it, she collects the undisputed part, raises a ticket for the charge and says the team will check (D3).
- She may state the charge amount only when it is in the customer facts. If it is not there, she says only that a charge applies after a bounce.
- Late payment affects the credit record. The number of days is to be confirmed, so she says only that a long delay affects the credit record.
